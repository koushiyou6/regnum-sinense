Introduction

A statute of the Yuelu Academy slips (volume IV, slips 329–331; the numbers in the text are the editors' slip numbers, register number and sequence number) from the reign of King Zhuangxiang: the Clerk of the Capital's report on the poor convict labour at the Western Workshop and the ruling on it.

[§2] 岳麓书院藏秦简

[§2] Qin slips in the Yuelu Academy collection

泰上皇时内史言：西工室司寇、隐官、践更多贫不能自给粮。议：令县遣司寇入禾，其县毋（无）禾0587/329当貣者，告作所县偿及贷。西工室伐榦沮、南郑山，令沮、南郑听西工室致。其入禾者及吏移西0638/330工室。·二年曰：复用。0681/331

In the time of the Grand Supreme Emperor1 the Clerk of the Capital said: The sikou, the yinguan and those serving their geng turn2 at the Western Workshop are many of them poor and cannot provide their own grain. Deliberation3: Let the Counties send the sikou to deliver grain4; where a County has no grain to be lent5, let it report to the County of the work-site6 for repayment and loan7. The Western Workshop fells timber in the hills of Ju and Nanzheng; let Ju and Nanzheng act on the Western Workshop's requisitions8. Those who deliver the grain, and the officers, are passed on9 to the Western Workshop. · In the second year it was said: Put back in use.10

Terms

Clerk of the Capital / 内史n. the office governing the home territory of Qin about the capital.
sikou / 司寇n. the lightest grade of Qin convict labour, 'robber-guard', ranking between commoners and the lichenqie 隶臣妾 slaves; by Han law a term of two years.
yinguan / 隐官n. a person pardoned after a mutilating punishment and set to work out of public sight, 'the hidden'; a status ranked with the sikou.
serving one's geng turn / 践更n. performing in person one's turn (更) of statutory labour service for the government, as against hiring a substitute.
the Western Workshop / 西工室n. a state workshop (工室) of Qin, staffed with convicts and men on labour service; the statute does not say where it stood. A Qin Western Workshop (西工) of Shu Commandery is attested on slips excavated at Zikujie, Chengdu.
County / 县n. the basic unit of direct administration, capitalized as an administrative unit.
officer / 吏n. a man in government service below the rank of official; a clerk or functionary.

Persons

King Zhuangxiang of Qin 秦庄襄王King of Qin, reigned 249–247 BC; personal name Zichu 子楚 (Suoyin).

Places

Ju 沮a County of Qin in Hanzhong Commandery, on the upper Ju River 沮水 (the Jialing); modern Lueyang 略阳, Shaanxi.
Nanzheng 南郑a County of Qin, the seat of Hanzhong Commandery, in the Han River valley; modern Hanzhong 汉中, Shaanxi.

Notes

1 Grand Supreme Emperor泰上皇, that is 太上皇, the title the First Emperor gave his father King Zhuangxiang after his death; "in the time of" the Grand Supreme Emperor is Zhuangxiang's reign, 249–247 BC, which places the statute here, at the reign's first year; the year within the reign is not given.
2 geng turn践更: the editors' pause marks make three groups, sikou, yinguan and men on their geng turn; Chen Wei reads 司寇、隐官践更, the sikou and yinguan on their geng turn, as in another Yuelu statute (slip 017). The editors' punctuation is kept.
3 Deliberation议: the ruling that answers the report (言); the form of a Qin statute of this kind.
4 deliver grain入禾: grain unhusked; the County supplies the grain its sikou will eat at the Workshop.
5 to be lent貣, the editors' reading 贷 kept in parentheses: 当貣者, the quantity due to be lent.
6 County of the work site作所县: the County in which the Workshop's work-site lies.
7 for repayment and loan偿及贷: as read here, the County of the work-site lends the grain and the sikou's own County repays it.
8 act on its requisitions听…致: 致, the note by which an office calls for men or goods to be delivered; 听, to comply with it.
9 passed on移: the forwarding of men and of the papers that go with them.
10 second year·二年曰：复用: an annotation after the scribe's dot, recording that the statute was put back in force in a second year; Chen Wei takes it as the second year of King Zheng, 245 BC.
