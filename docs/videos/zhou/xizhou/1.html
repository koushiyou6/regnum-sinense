约公元前1046年—公元前1036年

c. 1046–1036 BC

History of the Zhou Dynasty · Western Zhou · 西周

Introduction

The first years after the conquest of Shang, about 1046 to 1036 BC: King Wu's settlement of the royal domain of Yin Shang and his enfeoffments; the Duke of Zhou's regency, the rising of Guan, Cai and Wu Geng and the three years of the eastern campaign; the founding of Yan (燕) and the fall of Yan (奄) and Pugu; and the moving of the people of Yin to Luoyi, the Zhouyuan and Feng and Hao.

Annotations

[§1] 约公元前1046年—公元前1036年

[§1] c. 1046–1036 BC

帝辛死后，武王的下一步行动是实现对殷商王畿的彻底控制；据《逸周书》记载，武王在甲子日傍晚进入朝歌后，就派遣太公率军抵御帝辛的臣子方来。三天后的丁卯日，太公得胜归来，基本扑灭了殷商王畿的残余抵抗势力。

After Di Xin's death, King Wu's next step was to bring the royal domain of Yin Shang wholly under his control; according to the Yi Zhou Shu, King Wu, after entering Zhaoge on the evening of the day Jiazi, at once sent Taigong with an army to hold off Fang Lai, a minister of Di Xin. Three days later, on the day Dingmao, Taigong came back victorious, having for the most part put out what resistance remained in the royal domain of Yin Shang.

在殷墟王畿，除方来等残存抵抗势力，殷商的宗亲旧族，多无心与周军对抗；据《左传》记载，帝辛的庶兄微子启主动向武王请降，武王亲自替微子启松绑，收下他含着的玉璧，随后命他返回封邑微地。

In the royal domain of Yinxu, apart from Fang Lai and the other remnants of resistance, the kinsmen and old clans of Yin Shang for the most part had no heart to stand against the Zhou army; according to the Zuozhuan, Weizi Qi, Di Xin's elder brother by a secondary consort, came of his own accord to offer his surrender to King Wu, and King Wu with his own hands loosed Weizi Qi's bonds, accepted the jade disc he held in his mouth, and then ordered him back to his fief, the land of Wei (微).

据《逸周书·克殷》所载，武王在殷商王畿举行了社祭，把帝辛子武庚封在殷邑，命三弟管叔鲜辅佐；武王安排召公去释放被囚禁的箕子，又安排毕叔与康叔去释放了被帝辛囚禁的宗族族人，并命闳夭封比干之墓。武王征召殷商王畿的大小贵族，在殷邑郊外会面，武王对他们发表训诫，即《商誓》。

By the account of the Yi Zhou Shu, Conquest of Yin, King Wu held a sacrifice at the altar of the soil in the royal domain of Yin Shang, enfeoffed Wu Geng, Di Xin's son, at the settlement of Yin, and ordered his third brother Guanshu Xian to assist him; King Wu sent the Duke of Shao to release the imprisoned Jizi, sent Bishu and Kangshu to release the kinsmen whom Di Xin had imprisoned, and ordered Hong Yao to raise a mound over the tomb of Bigan. King Wu summoned the nobles great and small of the royal domain of Yin Shang to meet him outside the settlement of Yin, and delivered an admonition to them, which is the Speech to Shang.

陕西临潼出土的西周青铜器利簋铭文表明，武王在辛未日，也就是克商的七天后已经离开了殷商王畿，抵达阑师；据《逸周书·世俘》记载，武王令吕他征伐越戏方、侯来伐陈地的靡集、百弇伐韦、陈本伐磿、百韦伐宣方，新荒伐蜀，六路皆捷。在取得胜利后，武王率军返回镐京，于四月庚戌日举行了献俘仪式。辛亥日，祭祀上天，伐纣战争至此完成。

The inscription of the Li gui, a Western Zhou bronze unearthed at Lintong in Shaanxi, shows that on the day Xinwei, that is, seven days after the conquest of Shang, King Wu had already left the royal domain of Yin Shang and reached Lanshi; according to the Yi Zhou Shu, Great Capture, King Wu ordered Lü Ta to campaign against the Yuexifang, Hou Lai to attack Miji in the land of Chen, Bai Yan to attack Wei (韦), Chen Ben to attack Li (磿), Bai Wei to attack the Xuanfang and Xin Huang to attack Shu, and all six columns were victorious. After these victories King Wu led his army back to Haojing, and on Gengxu of April held the rite of presenting the captives. On the day Xinhai he sacrificed to Heaven, and with this the war against Zhou (纣) was complete.

武王克商后的统治较为短暂，至多三年。在此期间，武王对“先代之后”以及宗亲功臣进行了分封。

King Wu's rule after the conquest of Shang was fairly short, three years at the most. In this time King Wu made enfeoffments to the "descendants of the former dynasties" and to his kinsmen and ministers of merit.

在“先代之后”中，夏、殷两代王族后裔，共三国受封，合称为“三恪”；此“三恪”的“舜后”是陈国，“夏后”是杞国，至于“殷后”，初为受封于邶的帝辛子武庚。后武庚作乱被讨平，周公又命帝辛庶兄微子启“代殷后”，宋国便成为了“三恪”之一。除此之外，亦有如炎黄之后、尧后等受封。

Among the "descendants of the former dynasties", the descendants of the royal houses of the two dynasties of Xia and Yin, three states in all, were enfeoffed, and are together called the "Three Ke"; of these "Three Ke" the "descendants of Shun" are the state of Chen, the "descendants of Xia" the state of Qi (杞), and as for the "descendants of Yin", they were at first Wu Geng, Di Xin's son, enfeoffed at Bei. Later, when Wu Geng rose in revolt and was put down, the Duke of Zhou ordered Weizi Qi, Di Xin's elder brother by a secondary consort, to "take the place of the descendants of Yin", and the state of Song thus became one of the "Three Ke". Besides these, others too were enfeoffed, such as the descendants of Yandi and the Yellow Emperor, the descendants of Yao and others.

除管叔鲜在克商之初被派辅佐武庚之外，武王亦增派了另外两位胞弟，蔡叔度和霍叔处，设置“三监”以对殷民进行监督，并拆分了殷商王畿；周武王还把几位胞弟分封在曹、郜、郕等地，又封太公吕尚于营丘，讨平莱夷并建立齐国，在东土立足。

Besides Guanshu Xian, sent at the very beginning of the conquest of Shang to assist Wu Geng, King Wu also sent two more of his full brothers, Caishu Du and Huoshu Chu, setting up the "Three Guards" to keep watch over the people of Yin, and divided up the royal domain of Yin Shang; King Wu of Zhou also enfeoffed several of his full brothers at Cao, Gao, Cheng (郕) and other places, and enfeoffed Taigong, Lü Shang, at Yingqiu, who put down the Laiyi and founded the state of Qi (齐), gaining a foothold in the eastern lands.

至周初，姜姓人群成规模地进入泾河中、上游，与来自西周王畿腹地的周人在泾河干流与支流马莲河的甘肃灵台、崇信与宁县、合水、环县地区共存。

By the beginning of Zhou, people of the Jiang surname had moved in numbers into the middle and upper reaches of Jing River, and lived side by side with Zhou people from the heart of the Western Zhou royal domain in the districts of Lingtai, Chongxin, Ning County, Heshui and Huan County in Gansu, on the main stream of Jing River and its tributary Malian River.

武王去世后，其子成王即位，周公姬旦承武王遗命而“代行天子事”，发布命令皆称“王命”；正在东方监管殷民的管叔、蔡叔极其不满，随即展开了对抗。

After King Wu's death his son King Cheng came to the throne, and Ji Dan, Duke of Zhou, on King Wu's dying charge, "conducted the affairs of the Son of Heaven in his stead", issuing all his orders as "the King's command"; Guanshu and Caishu, who were then in the east overseeing the people of Yin, were greatly displeased, and at once set themselves against him.

至于霍叔，部分学者认为可能只是被两位兄长裹挟，并非动乱的主要发起者，所以早期的文献大多加以忽略；随着管蔡二人公然对抗周公，帝辛之子武庚也在殷邑起兵。

As for Huoshu, some scholars think he may only have been carried along by his two elder brothers and was not a chief mover of the troubles, which is why the early texts for the most part pass him over; as Guan and Cai openly set themselves against the Duke of Zhou, Wu Geng, Di Xin's son, also raised troops at the settlement of Yin.

传统说法大多认为管蔡与武庚联合作乱，但部分的学者根据清华简《系年》的记载推断管蔡起初或只单纯反对周公，并未勾结武庚，原殷商王畿地区的贵族、遗民看到周国宗室内讧，便杀死了留在殷邑的周人属吏、军士，拥戴武庚叛乱。

The traditional accounts for the most part hold that Guan and Cai rose in revolt in league with Wu Geng, but some scholars infer from the record of the Xinian of the Tsinghua slips that Guan and Cai at first perhaps simply opposed the Duke of Zhou and were not in collusion with Wu Geng; the nobles and remnant people of the former royal domain of Yin Shang, seeing the royal house of Zhou at strife within itself, killed the Zhou officers and soldiers left at the settlement of Yin and raised Wu Geng in revolt.

据《尚书大传》记载，奄国和蒲姑的君主亦有派人通告武庚举事。

According to the Shangshu Dazhuan, the rulers of the state of Yan (奄) and of Pugu also sent men to tell Wu Geng to rise.

管、蔡宣称“公将不利于孺子”，让周朝其他重臣乃至成王本人，对周公摄政心生怀疑；周公一方面极力陈述自己的用心良苦，另一方面表示绝不姑息作乱之人。

Guan and Cai gave out that "the Duke will do no good to the young son1", so that the other great ministers of the Zhou court, and even King Cheng himself, came to doubt the Duke of Zhou's regency; the Duke of Zhou on the one hand did his utmost to set out the pains he had taken, and on the other made it plain that he would show no indulgence to those who raised revolt.

周公东征的第一年，主要是在遏制管、蔡以及殷商旧族的攻势；周公坐镇洛地，其子伯禽则率军插入管叔封国的后方，而其他邦君诸侯也配合了周公的行动：温、檀两国扼控着从殷商王畿南下伊洛地区的交通要道，原、雍两国扼控着太行山南麓的隘口，姬姓黎国则镇守着太行山西侧的上党台地，在它们的封锁之下，殷商王畿的叛军也是一筹莫展。

The first year of the Duke of Zhou's eastern campaign was spent mainly in checking the offensive of Guan, Cai and the old clans of Yin Shang; the Duke of Zhou held the land of Luo in person, while his son Boqin led an army in behind Guanshu's fief, and the other rulers of states and Lords also acted in concert with the Duke of Zhou: the two states of Wen and Tan held the main road running south from the royal domain of Yin Shang to the region of Yi River and Luo River, the two states of Yuan and Yong held the Passes at the southern foot of Taihangshan, and the Ji-surnamed state of Li (黎) guarded the Shangdang Plateau on the western side of Taihangshan; under their blockade the rebel army of the royal domain of Yin Shang could do nothing.

在济水沿线，先前周军东拓的产物——齐、郕、曹、郜等国更是牵制了以奄国、蒲姑为代表的亲商方国，使它们无法有效支援殷商王畿。

Along Ji River, the states that the Zhou army's earlier advance to the east had produced, Qi (齐), Cheng (郕), Cao, Gao and others, moreover pinned down the pro-Shang fang states, of which the state of Yan (奄) and Pugu were the chief, so that they could give no effective support to the royal domain of Yin Shang.

在东征的第二年，周公彻底击败管、蔡，管叔或被处死、或自杀，蔡叔或被囚，或与霍叔同遭流放。

In the second year of the eastern campaign the Duke of Zhou utterly defeated Guan and Cai; Guanshu was either put to death or killed himself, and Caishu was either imprisoned or banished together with Huoshu.

在管、蔡败亡后，周公继而挥师攻克了殷邑，武庚向北逃窜；根据安阳小屯发掘所见的柱烬以及基址的红烧土，可以得知殷都在最终废弃时经历了火灾。殷墟王陵以及王陵区以外的绝大多数贵族墓葬也在周公东征的期间集体遭到了破坏性盗掘，晚商殷墟四期彻底结束。

After the defeat and ruin of Guan and Cai, the Duke of Zhou went on to lead his army against the settlement of Yin and took it, and Wu Geng fled north; from the charred posts and the burnt red earth of the foundations found in the excavations at Xiaotun, Anyang, it can be known that the capital of Yin went through a fire when it was finally abandoned. The royal tombs of Yinxu, and the great majority of the nobles' tombs outside the royal cemetery, were also plundered and wrecked together in the course of the Duke of Zhou's eastern campaign, and Late Shang Yinxu Phase IV came to a final end.

成王对周公的误会消除后，到东征的第三年便亲自参与了“践奄”的重大战役。另一方面，成王派遣太保召公奭率军讨伐北逃的武庚；尽管武庚被追上杀死，但仍然有一部分残党继续北逃，召公率军队追击，直抵燕地，经营据点。后来召公班师，成王就封召公之子克为匽侯。

Once King Cheng's misunderstanding of the Duke of Zhou was cleared away, in the third year of the eastern campaign he took part in person in the great campaign of the "trampling of Yan (奄)". On the other side, King Cheng sent the Grand Guardian Shi, Duke of Shao, with an army against Wu Geng, who had fled north; although Wu Geng was overtaken and killed, a part of his following still fled on northwards, and the Duke of Shao pursued them with his army as far as the land of Yan (燕), where he set up strongholds. Afterwards, when the Duke of Shao brought his army home, King Cheng enfeoffed the Duke of Shao's son Ke as Marquis of Yan2 (匽).

先前北京地区已有箕子宗族北迁建立的㠱国，根据考古铭文中匽侯赏赐㠱侯族人的内容来看，这一支殷商遗民与匽国的关系较为融洽；至于武庚之乱的残党，或许逃到了更靠北一点的地方，例如孤竹国，而西周初辽西遗存中的殷商式器物可能与其有关。

Before this there was already in the Beijing region the state of Ji (㠱), founded by Jizi's clan after its move north; to judge from the excavated inscriptions in which the Marquis of Yan (匽) makes gifts to kinsmen of the Marquis of Ji (㠱), this branch of the remnant people of Yin Shang was on fairly good terms with the state of Yan (匽); as for the remnants of Wu Geng's revolt, they perhaps fled to somewhere a little further north, for example the state of Guzhu, and the vessels of Yin Shang type among the remains of western Liaoning at the beginning of the Western Zhou may have to do with them.

奄国被攻破后，原本避难于此的帝辛之臣飞廉继续向东逃窜，周军追击到海边，将他杀死；成王又把包括飞廉族人在内的奄国遗民西迁到朱圉山，命令他们抵御“奴虘之戎”。

After the state of Yan (奄) was broken, Feilian, a minister of Di Xin who had taken refuge there, fled on eastwards, and the Zhou army pursued him to the edge of the sea and killed him; King Cheng also moved the remnant people of the state of Yan (奄), Feilian's kinsmen among them, west to Zhuyushan, and ordered them to hold off the "Rong of Nucuo".

消灭奄国以后，周公又率军征伐了东夷丰伯之国与蒲姑国；这两国应是在太公的配合下消灭的，所以后来都被成王增封给齐国。此后，周公广伐南国。

After destroying the state of Yan (奄), the Duke of Zhou also led his army against the Dongyi state of the Earl of Feng and the state of Pugu; these two states must have been destroyed with Taigong's cooperation, which is why both were afterwards added by King Cheng to the fief of the state of Qi (齐). After this the Duke of Zhou campaigned widely against the southern states.

几个主要的亲商方国覆亡后，东部地区的纪、薛等国也倒向了周人；据《逸周书》记载，周公还征服了东夷的“熊盈族十有七国”，不过，东夷的大国徐国似乎并未受到重创，后来周公之子伯禽迁封于东方，依然要和徐国斗争。

After the fall of the several chief pro-Shang fang states, Ji (纪), Xue and other states of the east also went over to the Zhou people; according to the Yi Zhou Shu, the Duke of Zhou also conquered "seventeen states of the Xiong and Ying clans" of the Dongyi; the great Dongyi state of Xu (徐), however, seems not to have been badly hurt, and later, when the Duke of Zhou's son Boqin was moved to a fief in the east, he still had to fight the state of Xu (徐).

东征胜利后，周公把一批拥戴武庚叛乱的殷商旧贵迁往九毕，即有重兵屯驻的洛地附近，以便在新都洛邑建成后可以就近管控；因为他们先前反抗周人的统治，也被称为“顽民”。

After the victory of the eastern campaign, the Duke of Zhou moved a body of the old nobility of Yin Shang who had raised Wu Geng in revolt to Jiubi, that is, near the land of Luo, where a strong force was stationed, so that once the new capital Luoyi was built they could be controlled from close at hand; because they had earlier resisted the rule of the Zhou people, they are also called the "refractory people3".

周公于摄政的第五年营建成周洛邑，其后有更多的殷商旧族被迁到附近安置。周公还重申了安抚政策，使有德之人会被选入王廷供职，而其他迁居洛邑的殷民，也能分到田地。

In the fifth year of his regency the Duke of Zhou built Chengzhou, Luoyi, after which still more of the old clans of Yin Shang were moved and settled near it. The Duke of Zhou also restated his policy of conciliation, by which men of virtue would be chosen to serve at the royal court, while the other people of Yin moved to Luoyi could also be given fields.

除了成周洛邑以外，还有部分殷商旧族被迁往周原、丰镐安置；与洛邑的“顽民”不同，这些旧族大多是愿意与周人合作的一派，早在武王克商之初，他们就被陆续迁往西土。

Besides Chengzhou, Luoyi, some of the old clans of Yin Shang were also moved and settled in the Zhouyuan and at Feng and Hao; unlike the "refractory people" of Luoyi, these old clans were for the most part the party willing to work with the Zhou people, and as early as the beginning of King Wu's conquest of Shang they were moved one after another to the western lands.

至于殷商王畿，仍有众多未被迁徙的遗民。周公让胞弟康叔封去管辖居留原地的殷商遗民，成王亲政后，嘉奖康叔的功劳，召他回朝担任司寇。

As for the royal domain of Yin Shang, many remnant people who had not been moved were still there. The Duke of Zhou had his full brother Kangshu Feng govern the remnant people of Yin Shang who stayed where they were, and after King Cheng took up the government himself he commended Kangshu's services and called him back to court to serve as Minister of Justice.

与殷商王畿情况有些类似，商奄的故地后来迁走了一批“顽民”后，还是有众多遗民居留原地，于是令伯禽迁居东方以巩固周公东征的胜利结果。但在许国都城附近，仍然留有一块鲁国的“飞地”许田，作为鲁国君主至洛朝觐时的临时驻地。

Much as in the royal domain of Yin Shang, in the old land of Shang Yan (商奄), after a body of "refractory people" had later been moved away, many remnant people still stayed where they were, and so Boqin was ordered to move to the east to make fast the fruits of the Duke of Zhou's eastern campaign. Near the capital of the state of Xu (许), however, there still remained Xutian, an "exclave" of the state of Lu, as a lodging for the rulers of Lu when they went to Luo to attend court.

Terms

royal domain / 王畿n. the land about the capital under the king's own rule.
altar of the soil / 社n. the altar of the god of the soil of a state or settlement.
Li gui / 利簋n. a bronze gui of the first years of the Western Zhou, unearthed at Lintong, Shaanxi, in 1976; its inscription dates the conquest of Shang to the morning of the day Jiazi.
the Yuexifang / 越戏方n. a fang state attacked by Lü Ta on King Wu's orders, in the Great Capture chapter of the Yi Zhou Shu.
the Xuanfang / 宣方n. a fang state attacked by Bai Wei on King Wu's orders, in the Great Capture chapter of the Yi Zhou Shu.
descendants of the former dynasties / 先代之后n. the heirs of earlier ruling houses, enfeoffed by King Wu.
Three Ke / 三恪n. the three houses of former dynasties enfeoffed and honoured by the Zhou: the descendants of Shun, of Xia and of Yin.
Three Guards / 三监n. the three brothers of King Wu set to watch over the people of Yin: Guanshu, Caishu and Huoshu.
the Laiyi / 莱夷n. the Yi people of the Shandong peninsula.
the eastern lands / 东土n. the lands east of the Zhou homeland.
Son of Heaven / 天子n. the emperor.
Tsinghua slips / 清华简n. the Warring States bamboo manuscripts held by Tsinghua University.
the Lords / 诸侯npl. the rulers of the states, collectively; in this period the kings of the warring states and the lesser lords, as distinct from the Zhou king.
fang state / 方n. the polities around the Shang, so named in the oracle-bone inscriptions.
Late Shang / 晚商n. the Yinxu period of Shang archaeology, from Pan Geng's move to Yin.
Yinxu Phase IV / 殷墟四期n. the last of the four phases of the Yinxu (Anyang) period, opening with the accession of Di Yi.
Grand Guardian / 太保n. one of the highest offices of the early Zhou court, held by the Duke of Shao.
the Rong of Nucuo / 奴虘之戎n. a Rong people of the west, in the Xinian.
the Dongyi / 东夷n. the Yi peoples of the east, in the transmitted texts.
the Xiong and Ying clans / 熊盈族n. peoples of the Dongyi conquered by the Duke of Zhou, in the Yi Zhou Shu.
refractory people / 顽民n. the people of Yin who had resisted the rule of Zhou and were moved to Luoyi.
the western lands / 西土n. the lands west of the Shang, as the oracle-bone inscriptions call them.
Minister of Justice / 司寇n. the Zhou office in charge of punishments.

Notes

1 the Duke will do no good to the young son公将不利于孺子: the Metal-bound Coffer of the Documents, after Legge, "the duke would do no good to the (king's) young son".
2 Marquis of Yan匽: the form of 燕, Yan, in the bronze inscriptions.
3 refractory people顽民: Legge's rendering in the Charge to the Duke of Bi of the Documents, 毖殷顽民，迁于洛邑, "Cautiously did he deal with the refractory people of Yin, and removed them to the city of Lo".
