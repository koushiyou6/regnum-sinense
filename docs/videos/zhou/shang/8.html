约公元前1150年—公元前1088年

c. 1150–1088 BC

History of the Zhou Dynasty · Shang · 商

Introduction

The reigns of Kang Ding, Wu Yi and Wen Ding, about 1150 to 1088 BC: the wars with the Qiangfang and the other fang states of the west; the move of the Zhou under Gugong Danfu from Bin to the Zhouyuan, and the accounts of Taibo and Zhongyong; the Shang court's turn to the east and the salt-making settlements of the Bohai coast; and Jili's campaigns against the Rong and Di, down to his imprisonment and death under Wen Ding.

Annotations

[§8] 约公元前1150年—公元前1088年

[§8] c. 1150–1088 BC

三期卜辞大部分与羌方交战的内容都属于廪辛之弟康丁统治时期；与武丁时期不同，康丁时期的卜辞少见甚至不见选将和登人聚众，反而多见“戍”与“行”投入战斗，这反映此时的军队已有较为固定的编制。除此之外，卜辞中还有康丁发动附属部族以及方国军队征伐羌方的记录。

Most of what the Yinxu Phase III inscriptions have on fighting with the Qiangfang belongs to the reign of Kang Ding, younger brother of Lin Xin; unlike those of Wu Ding's time, the inscriptions of Kang Ding's time seldom or never show the choosing of commanders and the levying and mustering of men, and show instead, again and again, the "garrisons" and the "columns" being sent into battle, which reflects an army that by now had a fairly fixed organisation. Besides this, the inscriptions also record Kang Ding calling out dependent tribes and the armies of fang states to attack the Qiangfang.

从康丁对羌方作战之前的卜问内容来看，不乏其对作战方略的详细考虑；另一方面，这些卜辞也反映了羌方侵袭的严重性，以至于康丁要谨慎行事，并多次征调戍军、臣属部族和方国增援西土。除羌方以外，康丁还要应对来自方方的侵扰，但面对方的侵扰，商王朝的手段以防御为主，这可能与当时的首要打击目标并非方而是羌方有关。

To judge from what Kang Ding divined about before fighting the Qiangfang, there was no lack of detailed thought on his part about the plan of campaign; on the other hand, these inscriptions also reflect how serious the Qiangfang's raids were, so much so that Kang Ding had to act with caution, and many times called up garrison troops, subject tribes and fang states to reinforce the western lands. Besides the Qiangfang, Kang Ding also had to deal with the raids of the Fang; but in the face of the Fang's raids the measures of the Shang court were mainly defensive, which may be because the chief target at the time was not the Fang but the Qiangfang.

除了应对羌方和方，康丁在位时还征讨过东南部的危方；这一时期，西部的虘方、辔方、絴方、兹方也改变了自武丁征伐后的友好态度，转而与商朝为敌，康丁未能将他们重新征服。

Besides dealing with the Qiangfang and the Fang, Kang Ding in his reign also campaigned against the Weifang in the south-east; in this period the Cuofang, the Peifang, the Xiangfang and the Zifang in the west also gave up the friendly attitude they had kept since Wu Ding's campaigns and turned against the Shang, and Kang Ding was unable to conquer them again.

康丁去世后，其子武乙即位；在康丁时入寇的西部方国中，辔方放弃敌对，转而臣服商王朝，虘方和兹方不见于武乙时期的卜辞，具体情况不明，羌方和絴方在武乙卜辞中各有一条被商军征讨的记录，其后絴方表示臣服。

After Kang Ding's death his son Wu Yi came to the throne; of the western fang states that had raided in Kang Ding's time, the Peifang gave up its hostility and submitted to the Shang court; the Cuofang and the Zifang are not seen in the inscriptions of Wu Yi's time, and what became of them is not known; the Qiangfang and the Xiangfang each have one record in Wu Yi's inscriptions of being attacked by the Shang army, after which the Xiangfang declared its submission.

武乙在位时期另一个比较重要的事件，是姬周族群的迁徙；根据传世文献记载，周人的先公——古公亶父与武乙所处年代大致相当，受“薰鬻戎狄”所迫，率领部众从豳地南迁至岐山脚下的周原，并在抵达后迎娶姜姓之女。亶父带领民众革除戎俗，并开垦荒地、建造城郭，又设置五官有司，完善政治制度，使周人日渐强大。后世亦追尊亶父为太王。

Another event of some importance in Wu Yi's reign was the migration of the Ji Zhou people; according to the transmitted texts, the Zhou people's former lord Gugong Danfu, who lived at roughly the same time as Wu Yi, was pressed by the "Xunyu Rong and Di" and led his people south from Bin to the Zhouyuan at the foot of Qishan, and after arriving took to wife a woman of the Jiang surname. Danfu led the people in doing away with Rong customs, opened up waste land and built Fortresses and outer walls, and set up the five offices with their officers, bringing the system of government to completion, so that the Zhou people grew stronger day by day. Later ages also honoured Danfu posthumously as King Tai.

考古表明，殷墟二、三期之交，分布于泾河中上游的碾子坡类型和孙家类型遗存向南传播，与漆水河流域的郑家坡文化融合。郑家坡文化于是得以迅速扩张，并与刘家文化因素趋同发展。至于豳地原有的孙家类型则为断泾二期所取代。

Archaeology shows that at the turn from Yinxu Phase II to Phase III the remains of the Nianzipo type and the Sunjia type, distributed on the middle and upper reaches of Jing River, spread south and merged with the Zhengjiapo culture of the Qishui River valley. The Zhengjiapo culture was thereby able to expand rapidly, and developed in convergence with elements of the Liujia culture. As for the Sunjia type that had been in Bin, it was replaced by Duanjing Phase II.

《史记·周本纪》称周太王有三个儿子，长子太伯、次子仲雍、少子季历，其中季历是太王的正妃太姜所生；《列女传》则认为太伯、仲雍、季历三兄弟的生母都是太姜。季历娶太任为妻，生子昌，即是后来的周文王。

The Shiji, Basic Annals of Zhou says that King Tai of Zhou had three sons, the eldest Taibo, the second Zhongyong and the youngest Jili, of whom Jili was born of King Tai's principal consort Tai Jiang; the Lienüzhuan1, however, holds that Tai Jiang was the mother of all three brothers, Taibo, Zhongyong and Jili. Jili took Tai Ren to wife, and she bore him a son, Chang, who was the later King Wen of Zhou.

按《史记》的记载，太王欲立季历为储君，以传位于昌，于是太伯和仲雍主动出奔至南方的蛮夷之地，并以“句吴”为号，建立吴国；部分学者认为太伯和仲雍并没有逃到南方的蛮夷之地，而是统帅部分的周族，在山西的平陆建立了虞国，以筹划翦灭殷商。另一批学者则不认同这种观点，并主张太伯、仲雍最初立国的地点在江西赣江流域，在西周初改封到江苏宁镇地区，其分支则被迁回中原，另封虞国。

By the account of the Shiji, King Tai wished to make Jili his heir so that the throne might pass to Chang, and so Taibo and Zhongyong of their own accord fled to the lands of the barbarians in the south, took "Gouwu" as their style, and founded the state of Wu; some scholars hold that Taibo and Zhongyong did not flee to the lands of the barbarians in the south at all, but led a part of the Zhou people and founded the state of Yu at Pinglu in Shanxi, in order to plan the destruction of Yin Shang. Another group of scholars does not accept this view, and maintains that the place where Taibo and Zhongyong first founded their state was in the Ganjiang valley in Jiangxi, that at the beginning of the Western Zhou they were re-enfeoffed in the Ning–Zhen region of Jiangsu, and that a branch of them was moved back to the Central Plains and separately enfeoffed as the state of Yu.

部分学者指出，虽然卜辞中并没有商周的直接冲突的记录，但仍然能找到一些“间接证据”，如武乙曾征伐过西部的召方，或在西周初年召公奭的采邑召城，或在更北部的絴方、方方附近。

Some scholars point out that although the inscriptions have no record of direct conflict between Shang and Zhou, some "indirect evidence" can still be found: Wu Yi, for instance, campaigned against the Shaofang of the west, which lay either at Shao Fortress, the fief of Shi, Duke of Shao, in the first years of the Western Zhou, or further north, near the Xiangfang and the Fang.

除了应对西部敌对方国的侵扰外，本阶段的商王朝还征伐过徐、淮地区的夷方；这些军事行动在规模上虽不如帝辛时的征战，但也可被视作商王朝重新将战略重心转向东方的前兆。

Besides dealing with the raids of the hostile fang states of the west, the Shang court in this stage also campaigned against the Yifang of the Xu and Huai region; these operations did not match in scale the campaigns of Di Xin2's time, but they can still be seen as a foreshadowing of the Shang court's turning the weight of its strategy to the east once more.

除此之外，鲁北地区也是此时商王朝在东部用兵的重点区域；考古表明，殷墟三期晚段，鲁北地区的珍珠门文化分布范围有所退缩，商文化则向东扩展到了弥河的流域，以青州苏埠屯墓葬群为典型代表。部分学者推测，该地便是传世文献所载的蒲姑国。

Besides this, the northern Shandong region too was a chief area of the Shang court's use of arms in the east at this time; archaeology shows that in the late stage of Yinxu Phase III the range of the Zhenzhumen culture in northern Shandong shrank somewhat, while Shang culture extended east as far as the Mi River valley, of which the Subutun cemetery at Qingzhou is the typical example. Some scholars conjecture that this place is the state of Pugu recorded in the transmitted texts.

在包含鲁北和沧州东部地区的渤海沿岸，横跨二百五十公里的范围内发现了十多个盐业聚落群，每个聚落群由十几处至几十处制盐作坊组成，规模宏大；部分学者认为该阶段的商王朝经略东部地区、开辟水陆通道，很可能是为了控制鲁北海盐和保障长江下游的铜、锡原料顺利北运。可见，晚商三期的商王朝经略重点转向东方，应该亦有出于对经济效益的考量。

Along the Bohai coast, taking in northern Shandong and the eastern part of the Cangzhou region, more than ten clusters of salt-making settlements have been found over a stretch of two hundred and fifty kilometres, each cluster made up of from a dozen or so to several dozen salt workshops, on a grand scale; some scholars hold that the Shang court's management of the east in this stage, and its opening of routes by water and land, were very probably meant to control the sea salt of northern Shandong and to keep the copper and tin of the lower Yangtze moving north without hindrance. It can be seen that when the Shang court of Late Shang Phase III turned the weight of its effort to the east, considerations of economic gain must also have played a part.

武乙在西狩时“雷暴震死”；部分学者认为这可能与其西征召方失利有关。此后，其子文丁继位。

Wu Yi, on a hunt in the west, was "struck dead in a thunderstorm3"; some scholars think this may be connected with the failure of his western campaign against the Shaofang. After this his son Wen Ding succeeded to the throne.

尽管卜辞显示康丁、武乙这两代商王在抗击西部的敌对方国的战争中取得了一定成果，但在考古层面上，却并未发现有商文化据点随着这些军事胜利而重新出现在西安以西；晋中地区遗址的商文化因素亦在殷墟三期以后逐渐消退，石楼类型的影响力则重新加强，正是在此时期，周人开始扩展势力范围。

Although the inscriptions show that Kang Ding and Wu Yi, two generations of Shang kings, had some success in their wars against the hostile fang states of the west, on the archaeological level no strongholds of Shang culture are found reappearing west of Xi'an in the wake of these victories; the Shang cultural elements at the sites of the central Shanxi region likewise faded away by degrees after Yinxu Phase III, while the influence of the Shilou type grew strong again; and it was in just this period that the Zhou people began to extend their sphere of power.

考古表明，先周文化因素在殷墟三期后出现于晋陕高原的众多遗址中，甚至传播到了晋中盆地。部分学者认为此种现象可能与季历征伐戎狄相关；季历在渭河沿线用兵，传世文献中只有攻灭毕程氏一则记录。季历其他见载的征伐行动，基本上都是针对晋陕高原的戎狄部族。

Archaeology shows that after Yinxu Phase III elements of Proto-Zhou culture appear at many sites on the Shanxi–Shaanxi plateau, and spread even as far as the central Shanxi basin. Some scholars think this may be connected with Jili's campaigns against the Rong and Di; of Jili's use of arms along Wei River the transmitted texts have only one record, the destruction of the Bicheng clan. Jili's other recorded campaigns were nearly all directed against the Rong and Di tribes of the Shanxi–Shaanxi plateau.

文丁在位的第二年，季历继续征伐狄戎，以鬼戎为踏板讨伐燕京戎，但以失败告终。隔年，季历又伐余无之戎，获得胜利，被文丁命为牧师；接下来的数年内，季历称王，反复在山西地区讨伐戎狄，终让商王朝感到了威胁，被文丁囚禁而死。

In the second year of Wen Ding's reign Jili went on with his campaigns against the Di and Rong; using the Gui Rong as a stepping-stone he attacked the Yanjing Rong, but this ended in defeat. Two years later Jili attacked the Rong of Yuwu as well and was victorious, and was appointed mushi4 by Wen Ding; in the years that followed Jili took the title of King and campaigned again and again against the Rong and Di in the Shanxi region, until at last the Shang court felt threatened, and he was imprisoned by Wen Ding and died.

the Weifang / 危方n. a fang state of the south-east, attacked by Kang Ding.
the Cuofang / 虘方n. a fang state of the west, friendly after Wu Ding's campaigns and hostile again under Kang Ding.
the Peifang / 辔方n. a fang state west of Yin, bordering the Qiongfang, attacked by Wu Ding and Que.
the Xiangfang / 絴方n. a fang state of the west, hostile under Kang Ding, which submitted under Wu Yi.
the Zifang / 兹方n. a fang state of the west, friendly after Wu Ding's campaigns and hostile again under Kang Ding.
the Xunyu Rong and Di / 薰鬻戎狄n. the Xunyu, the northern people who in the transmitted texts pressed on the Zhou under King Tai; written 薰育 in the Basic Annals of Zhou and 獯鬻 in the Mencius.
Nianzipo type / 碾子坡类型n. a type of remains on the middle and upper Jing River named from the Nianzipo site, Changwu, Shaanxi.
Sunjia type / 孙家类型n. the cultural type of the upper Jing valley named from the Sunjia site, Binzhou.
Zhengjiapo culture / 郑家坡文化n. the late Shang-period culture of the Qishui valley named from the Zhengjiapo site, Wugong, Shaanxi.
Liujia culture / 刘家文化n. the Late Shang-period culture of the western Guanzhong named from the Liujia cemetery, Fufeng, Shaanxi; regarded by many scholars as the culture of the Qiangfang.
Duanjing Phase II / 断泾二期n. the remains named from the second phase of the Duanjing site, Binzhou, Shaanxi.
the Shaofang / 召方n. a fang state of the west attacked by Wu Yi.
the Yifang / 夷方n. a fang state of the east, against which Fu Zi campaigned.
Zhenzhumen culture / 珍珠门文化n. the native culture of northern Shandong in the Late Shang period, named from the Zhenzhumen site, Changdao, Shandong.
Late Shang / 晚商n. the Yinxu period of Shang archaeology, from Pan Geng's move to Yin.
Shilou type / 石楼类型n. the late Shang bronze-bearing remains of the Shanxi–Shaanxi uplands named from Shilou County, Shanxi, which some connect with the Qiongfang.
Proto-Zhou culture / 先周文化n. the archaeological culture of the Zhou before the conquest of Shang.
the Rong and Di / 戎狄n. the non-Zhou peoples of the west and north in the transmitted texts.
the Bicheng clan / 毕程氏n. a people on Wei River destroyed by Jili, in the transmitted texts.
the Gui Rong / 鬼戎n. a Rong people attacked by Jili; the 西落鬼戎 of the Bamboo Annals.
the Yanjing Rong / 燕京戎n. a Rong people against whom Jili was defeated; the 燕京之戎 of the Bamboo Annals.
the Rong of Yuwu / 余无之戎n. a Rong people defeated by Jili.
mushi / 牧师n. the title conferred on Jili by Wen Ding, in the Bamboo Annals.
